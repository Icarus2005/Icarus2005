## Building now: BrandSprk

**[BrandSprk](https://www.brandsprk.io/)** is an AI-powered brand and creative decision platform. It helps businesses and creative professionals work from brand context and a clear brief toward an explained creative recommendation.

**Why it matters:** AI makes creative production easier. Choosing what to create, why it fits a brand, and how to defend that choice is still difficult, especially for smaller teams.

**Initial customer focus:** Small and mid-sized creative or marketing agencies serving SME clients.

**First repeatable workflow:** Bring a real client brief and brand context into one workspace, compare creative directions, document the trade-offs, and prepare a client-facing recommendation. The next client brief creates a reason to return.

**Current stage:** Public beta, gathering feedback and preparing a controlled, founder-led commercial rollout. Paid adoption, repeat use and retention remain milestones to validate, not established results.

**Long-term thesis:** Retained brand context and accumulated decision history could make subsequent briefs faster and more consistent. We intend to test whether this creates repeat usage, switching value and a defensible workflow.
